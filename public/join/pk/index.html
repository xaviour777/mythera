    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    Do I need filmmaking or AI experience?

    No film degree needed. The Drama Method starts with story and audience psychology, and the AI tools are taught stage by stage while you build your pilot.

    How does a sprint work?

    Each of the six weekly sprints covers two stages of the Drama Method and ends with something you post. On Friday’s Premiere Night the best work is screened live and broken down by Zahid.

    What will I have by the end of the cohort?

    A published pilot, a World Bible for your characters, the pilot in three languages, a week of pod collabs across four platforms, and an Episode 2 plan you pitch on Demo Day.

    What is the difference between Room, Studio and Silver Button?

    Room includes the full course, your pod and the Premiere Nights. Studio adds 1:1 sessions with Zahid Iqbal and a personal review of your work every week. The Silver Button Sprint includes everything in Studio and does not stop at 42 days: we keep working with you until your channel reaches 100K subscribers and the Silver Play Button arrives.

    What is the Ship Guarantee?

    Submit all six weekly deliverables, and if your pilot isn’t published by Demo Day, your seat in the next cohort is free.
